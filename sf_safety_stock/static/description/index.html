Safety Stock Optimizer

Optimal safety stock and reorder points computed from real demand &mdash; never run out again.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Guessing safety stock levels leads to stockouts, lost sales and cash frozen in overstocked shelves.

Safety stock is usually a hunch, not a number. Without demand analysis, planners overstock expensive items and run out of critical ones. Odoo has no native safety stock optimizer &mdash; this module fills that gap.

Everything you need

📊

🔗

How much to order to reach target level.
🎯

Service level
