Dynamic Process Routing

Auto-select the optimal production route based on capacity, quality, cost, and lead time.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Production follows fixed routes even when better alternatives exist &mdash; wasting capacity and missing delivery dates.

Products often have multiple valid production paths, but MRP uses only one. When a workcenter is overloaded or quality drops, the system cannot automatically switch to a faster, cheaper, or higher-quality route. This module adds condition-based dynamic routing.
