Corporate Gifts & Hospitality Register

Anti-bribery register of gifts and hospitality for compliance teams.

Features

  • Declarations of gifts and hospitality given or received.
  • Approval threshold with automatic requirement detection.
  • Approval workflow managed by compliance managers.
  • Annual register per employee.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Declaration: Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Archived.
  • Below threshold: submission auto-approves the declaration.
