Shareholder Register & Capital Management (Cap Table)

Track shareholders, share classes and capital movements.

Features

  • Shareholder register with partner and function.
  • Share classes with nominal value and authorized shares.
  • Buy/sell capital movements with quantity and unit price.
  • Automatic movement amounts and issued shares.
  • Total shares and capital value per shareholder.
  • Sell validation against current holdings.
  • Posting workflow with immutable posted movements.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Capital Movement: Draft, Posted, Cancelled.
  • Issued shares and cap table updated from posted movements.
