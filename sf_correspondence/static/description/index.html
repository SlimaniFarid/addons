Business Mail & Correspondence Register

Track inbound and outbound correspondence for administrative and legal teams.

Features

  • Incoming and outgoing correspondence register.
  • Routing to departments and responsible people.
  • Response deadlines with automatic reminders.
  • Registered mail and acknowledgment tracking.
  • Scanned attachments.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Correspondence: Draft, Open, In Progress, Responded, Archived, Cancelled.
  • Daily cron creates reminder activities for due responses.
