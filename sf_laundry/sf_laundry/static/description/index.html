Laundry & Dry Cleaning Management

Laundry management for laundromat managers and dry cleaners.

Features

  • Deposit vouchers with customer details and item lists.
  • Item-level statuses: received, in progress, ready, delivered, lost.
  • Per-piece pricing by item type and service.
  • Automatic expected delivery date.
  • Pickup and delivery timestamps.
  • Daily alerts for overdue orders and slow items.
  • Customer history per partner.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Order: Draft, Received, In Progress, Ready, Delivered, Cancelled.
  • Item: Received, In Progress, Ready, Delivered, Lost.
