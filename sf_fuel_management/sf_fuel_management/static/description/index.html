Fuel & Fleet Management

Fuel cards, fills with L/100km consumption tracking, tanks with receipts and anomaly alerts.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Fuel purchases and vehicle consumption are tracked in spreadsheets &mdash; theft and abnormal consumption go unnoticed.

This module solves it with fuel cards, automatic L/100km consumption, tank gauges and anomaly alerts.

How it works

1

Create vehicles & cards

Register the fleet and assign a fuel card per vehicle.
2

Record fills

Volume, price and odometer are entered; totals and consumption are computed.
3

Monitor tanks

Track current level and receipts per site.
4

Review alerts & reports

Abnormal consumption and expiring cards are flagged; print monthly PDF reports.
