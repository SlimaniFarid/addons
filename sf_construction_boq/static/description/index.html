Construction BOQ & Subcontractor Billing

Bill of quantities, subcontract management and payment certificates — built for construction.

Odoo 18 Odoo 19 99.75 € OPL-1

Construction billing is messy: quantities change, subcontractors need tracking, and payment certificates must reflect work actually done. This module gives you a clear BOQ, subcontract management and automated payment certificates computed from certified work.

Why you need it

The problem

  • BOQs tracked in spreadsheets with no link to billing
  • Subcontractor commitments and payments hard to follow
  • Payment certificates computed by hand, error-prone
  • No audit trail of certified vs billed amounts

The solution

  • Structured BOQ with lines, quantities, unit prices and totals
  • Subcontracts linked to BOQ lines with commitments
  • Payment certificates with automated net / retention / paid amounts
  • Full workflow: draft, confirmed, in progress, paid, cancelled

Key features

📋

Bill of Quantities

Create detailed BOQs with lines, quantities, unit prices, and automatic totals. Numbered per year.

🤝

Subcontractors

Track subcontracts, their amounts and statuses, all linked to the BOQ and to certificates.

🧾

Payment certificates

Generate certificates from subcontracts with certified amounts, retention, nets and amounts to pay.

🧮

Automatic computation

Retention, previous certified amounts, current totals and net payable computed for you.

📄

PDF certificate report

A clean, printable payment certificate document for your client and your records.

🔀

Full workflows

BOQ and subcontract draft-to-done states; certificates draft-to-paid with cancellation.

Who it's for

  • General and specialist contractors
  • Quantity surveyors and project managers
  • Small construction firms moving off spreadsheets
  • Companies billing clients by certified work

How it works

  1. Install the module — a dedicated Construction menu appears.
  2. Create a BOQ with lines, quantities and unit prices; confirm it.
  3. Add subcontracts linked to the BOQ and confirm them.
  4. Generate a payment certificate from a subcontract.
  5. Print the certificate PDF and share it with your client.
  6. Track certified, retained and paid amounts across the project.
