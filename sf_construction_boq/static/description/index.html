Construction BOQ & Subcontractor Billing

Bill of Quantities, subcontract management and progress billing (IPC) for construction companies.

Stop managing construction estimates and progress certificates in Excel. Build multi-discipline Bills of Quantities, manage subcontracts with retention and advance, and issue interim payment certificates with automatic calculations.

Key Features

  • Bill of Quantities — Multi-discipline itemized estimates with code, description, product, quantity, unit price
  • BOQ Workflow — Draft → Confirmed → In Progress → Done / Cancelled
  • Subcontract Management — Contractor, contract amount, retention rate, advance, dates
  • Subcontract Workflow — Draft → Confirmed → In Progress → Closed / Cancelled
  • Progress Billing (IPC) — Period certificates with previous and current quantities
  • Automatic Calculations — Previous certified, current amount, retention, net amount, amount to pay
  • Certificate Workflow — Draft → Confirmed → Paid / Cancelled
  • PDF Report — Printable payment certificate with totals

Workflow

  1. Create a Bill of Quantities with itemized lines per discipline
  2. Confirm the BOQ and start the project
  3. Create subcontracts with retention rate and advance
  4. Generate payment certificates from a subcontract
  5. Enter period work done and confirm the certificate
  6. Print the certificate or mark it paid

Compatibility

Odoo 18.0 and 19.0. Works with Project, Product, and Accounting modules.
