Time &amp; Attendance System
==========================

Advanced time and attendance tracking in Odoo: shifts, overtime, late
arrivals and monthly summaries.

Features
--------
* Employee shift patterns per weekday
* Automatic overtime computation vs shift
* Late arrival and early departure detection
* Monthly attendance summary per employee
* Expected hours from shift patterns

Requirements
------------
* Odoo 18.0 / 19.0 with Employees (hr) and Attendances (hr_attendance).

Installation
------------
* Copy the module folder into your addons path.
* Install "Time &amp; Attendance System".

Usage
-----
1. Define shift patterns under *Employees > Time &amp; Attendance > Shifts*.
2. Check-ins and check-outs are analyzed automatically: expected hours,
   shift overtime and lateness are computed.
3. Use *Monthly Summaries* to review worked hours, overtime and late days.
