Time & Attendance System

Shifts, overtime, late arrivals and monthly summaries &mdash; automatically.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Punctuality and overtime tracked in spreadsheets leave payroll guessing and HR chasing paperwork.

Shift schedules, late arrivals and overtime are usually logged by hand, then reconciled at month end. Native Odoo shows raw check-ins only. This module adds shift patterns, expected hours, overtime and monthly summaries &mdash; automatically.
