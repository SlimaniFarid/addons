Automated Supplier Invoice Control

3-way matching of invoices, receipts and purchase orders with tolerance control.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Supplier invoice discrepancies hide in manual approvals &mdash; overpayments and errors go unnoticed.

This module solves it with a dedicated register, automatic checks, alerts and a follow-up dashboard.

🔍

3-Way Matching

Exception Handling

Match Score

Automatic score and status per invoice.
📈

Control Dashboard

Inbox of exceptions and daily review cron.

How it works

1

Receive the invoice

The invoice is automatically evaluated on posting.
2

Match runs

Quantities and prices are compared with tolerances.
3

Handle exceptions

Minor flagged, major blocks posting/payment.
4

Reconcile & arbitrate

AP manager arbitrates; daily cron re-checks.
