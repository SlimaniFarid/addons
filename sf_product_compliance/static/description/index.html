Product Compliance & Technical Documentation

Regulations, product requirements, compliance dossiers and certificates with expiry alerts.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Without proof of conformity, market access is blocked — expired certificates, ignored country requirements and incomplete technical files fail audits.

This module solves it with a regulations reference, product requirements, compliance dossiers and certificate expiry alerts.

Everything you need

📜

Regulations Reference

CE, RoHS, REACH, UL, FDA with market and mandatory flag.
🔖

Requirements per Product

Evidence and satisfied / not satisfied tracking.

📁

Compliance Dossiers

Documents, versioning and validation workflow.
⏰

Certificate Expiry Alerts

Daily cron expires certificates and raises activities.

✅

Computed Product Status

Compliant only when all dossiers are compliant.
📈

Compliance Dashboard

Dossiers by state and regulation.

How it works

1

Configure regulations

Define the markets and regulations (CE, RoHS, REACH...).
2

Attach requirements

Link the compliance requirements to each product.
3

Validate dossiers

Requirements must be satisfied before a dossier is compliant.
4

Track certificates

The cron expires them and alerts the compliance team.
