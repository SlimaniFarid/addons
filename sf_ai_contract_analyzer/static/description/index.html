AI Contract Analyzer & Obligations

Extract obligations, dates, risks from contracts with AI

  • AI Extraction — Mistral, Gemini, Claude, OpenAI (BYOK)
  • Key Entities — Parties, dates, auto-renewal, notice periods
  • Financial Terms — Value, payment terms, penalties, escalation
  • Obligations — SLAs, delivery, reporting, compliance, insurance
  • Risk Flags — Auto-renewal, unlimited liability, jurisdiction
  • Calendar Alerts — Renewals, notice deadlines, price reviews

  • Clause Comparison — Benchmark vs templates
  • Multi-Lang — Any language → English summary
