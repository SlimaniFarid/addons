Traceability & Batch Recall

Full batch traceability, recall events and product history &mdash; end to end.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Without lot-level traceability, a recall can take days and cost thousands &mdash; or worse, a recall never happens at all.

Regulated industries must trace every batch to every customer. Native Odoo shows raw lot moves but offers no recall workflow, no affected-customer list and no quality status. This module adds all of it &mdash; in one click.
