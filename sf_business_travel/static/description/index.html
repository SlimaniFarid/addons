Corporate Business Travel Management

Travel requests, approvals and itineraries for employers.

Features

  • Travel requests with budget and justification.
  • Hierarchical approval workflow.
  • Itinerary lines with estimated costs.
  • Budget versus estimated cost comparison.
  • Automatic departure reminders.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Travel: Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled.
  • Daily cron raises departure reminders for upcoming trips.
