IT Asset & License Manager

Track your entire IT fleet — hardware, assignments, licenses and renewals — with alerts before anything expires.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Lost laptops, unassigned assets and expired licenses — IT chaos costs real money.

IT teams juggle spreadsheets to track hardware, software licenses and assignments. Nothing sends a reminder when a license expires or an asset disappears. This module brings a structured asset and license lifecycle directly into Odoo.

How it works

1

Add your assets & licenses

Create categories, register hardware and license keys.
2

Assign to employees

Use the wizard for a clean, traceable assignment.
3

Watch expiration

Licenses about to expire are flagged automatically.
4

Release & reassign

Returned assets go back to the available pool.
