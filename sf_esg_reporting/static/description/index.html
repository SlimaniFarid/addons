ESG Reporting (CSRD)

Collect, validate and report ESG indicators per company and period for CSRD compliance.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

ESG indicators are collected in spreadsheets without audit trail, variation proof or target tracking &mdash; CSRD reporting is at risk.

This module solves it with a configurable indicator repository, validated periods and automatic variation and target achievement.

Everything you need

📄

Indicator Repository

Configurable E/S/G indicators with unit, direction and frequency.
📅

Periods & Workflow

draft -> submitted -> approved -> closed, manager validation.

📈

Variation & Targets

Automatic previous-period variation and value/target achievement.
📦

Regulatory Reports

ESG PDF report per company and period + CSV export.

🏫

Multi-company

Values and periods per company with record rules.
📈

Dashboard

Values by indicator category.

How it works

1

Define indicators

Environment, social and governance KPIs with unit and target.
2

Collect values per period

Enter values per company, indicator and period.
3

Submit & validate

The manager approves; variation and achievement are computed.
4

Export reports

Generate the ESG PDF report and the CSV export.
