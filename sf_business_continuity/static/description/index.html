Business Continuity & BIA (PCA)

Resilience ISO 22301: critical processes BIA, continuity strategies, recovery plans, exercises and review alerts.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

After a disaster, companies without a BIA and recovery plans lose their critical activities and revenue.

This module solves it with criticality assessment, recovery plans, exercises and automatic review reminders.

Everything you need

📈

Business Impact Analysis

Criticality, RTO / RPO and financial impact per process.
🛠️

Continuity Strategies

Alternate site, workaround, outsourcing, manual, staffing.

📁

Recovery Plans

Steps, owners, resources and a publish / test / update workflow.
🎯

Exercises & Tests

Scenarios, objectives, results and improvement findings.

⏰

Review Alerts

Daily cron reminds the periodic review of plans.
📄

PDF Reports

BIA and Recovery Plan printed reports.

How it works

1

Assess critical processes

Register processes with criticality, RTO, RPO and impact.
2

Define strategies & plans

Describe continuity strategies and recovery steps per process.
3

Publish & test

Publish requires summary and steps; run exercises and record results.
4

Keep plans up to date

The daily cron reminds each plan review before it expires.
