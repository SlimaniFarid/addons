Equipment Rental & Hire Operations

Professional rental management for equipment hire companies.

Features

  • Equipment cards with tiered prices.
  • Rental contracts with calendar availability.
  • Availability conflict detection.
  • Out/in inspections with damages and penalties.
  • Maintenance planning between rentals.
  • Automatic invoicing of returned contracts.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Contract: Draft, Confirmed, Active, Returned, Invoiced, Closed, Cancelled.
  • Inspection: Out / In with condition and damages.
  • Maintenance: Draft, Scheduled, In Progress, Done, Cancelled.
