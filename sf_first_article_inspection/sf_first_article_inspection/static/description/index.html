First Article Inspection (FAI)

AS9102/AS9145 compliant first article inspection &mdash; characteristic accountability, balloon drawings, and approval workflows.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

First article inspections live in spreadsheets and PDFs, with no traceability from drawing to disposition.

Aerospace and automotive suppliers need full characteristic accountability per AS9102/AS9145. Balloon numbers, measured values, non-conformances, and approvals are scattered across files. This module brings FAI into Odoo Quality.
