Parking Lot & Garage Management

Parking lot and garage management for parking operators.

Features

  • Sites, zones and places with capacity and occupation states.
  • Entry/exit tickets with hourly and daily rates.
  • Recurring subscriptions with expiry reminders.
  • Occupancy and revenue statistics per site.
  • Automatic daily subscription checks.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Ticket: Draft, Open, Closed, Paid, Cancelled.
  • Subscription: Draft, Active, Expired, Cancelled.
  • Place: Free, Occupied, Reserved, Out of Service.
