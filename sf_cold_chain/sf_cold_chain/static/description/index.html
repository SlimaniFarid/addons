Cold Chain Monitoring

Temperature excursion monitoring for storage sites and refrigerated transport.

Features

  • Cold storage sites and transport trips with temperature ranges.
  • Readings with automatic range check.
  • Automatic excursion detection and reuse of open excursions.
  • Duration, maximum deviation and severity analytics.
  • Manager-only resolution with notes.
  • Escalation alerts for unresolved excursions.
  • Cold chain log PDF.
  • Multi-company support.
