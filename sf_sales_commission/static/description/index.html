Sales Commission Engine

Compute and manage sales commissions automatically with flexible, transparent rules.

Key Features

  • Flat, margin-based and tiered commission plans
  • Product / category rate overrides
  • Automatic generation from confirmed sale orders
  • Manual adjustments and clawbacks
  • Approve / Paid / Cancelled workflow
  • Per-period and per-salesperson totals

Works with native Odoo Sales. Install and open the Sales Commissions menu.
