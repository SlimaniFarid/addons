# Sales Commission Engine

Compute and manage sales commissions automatically with flexible, transparent rules.

## Features

- Commission plans with flat rate, percentage-of-margin, and tiered rates
- Product / product-category rate overrides
- Commission earned on the paid invoice amount
- Automatic commission line generation from confirmed sale orders
- Manual adjustments (prorations, bonuses, clawbacks)
- Draft / Approved / Paid / Cancelled workflow with manager approval
- Per-period and per-salesperson totals
- Commission summary shown directly on the sale order

## Installation

Install the module from Apps. Open the **Sales** app then **Sales Commissions**.

## Usage

1. **Sales > Sales Commissions > Commission Plans**: define a plan with
   rate, calculation type (gross or margin), optional tiers and overrides.
2. **Sales > Sales Commissions > Commission Lines**: lines appear here once
   generated. Approve, pay or cancel them.
3. On a confirmed quotation, press the **Generate Commission** button
   (order form, header) to create the commission line.
