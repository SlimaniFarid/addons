FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before installing.

Are accruals posted to the ledger?
Accruals are computed amounts per month; posting the accrual entry stays in your process (v1.1 adds auto entries).
Can a deal cover only one category?
Yes - scope by product category or leave empty for all.
What if the vendor disputes the claim?
Mark the claim Disputed and track resolution.
