Trade Promotion Management (TPM)

Manage trade promotions for FMCG and wholesale: promotion programs with budgets, periods and eligible customers, customer claims recording and validation, budget consumption tracking and ROI per program.

Features

  • Promotion programs with budget, type, period and eligible customers.
  • Customer claims with submission, approval, rejection and payment workflow.
  • Budget consumption tracking (total claimed, remaining budget).
  • ROI per program (budget utilization percentage).
  • Daily cron closing programs past their end date.
  • Validation threshold with review activities.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Program: Draft, Active, Closed, Cancelled.
  • Claim: Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Paid, Cancelled.
