User Manual

Step-by-step guide

Everything you need to get started.

1
Install the module

Copy sf_lease_ifrs16 to your addons path, restart Odoo, then go to Apps and click Install.

2
Map your accounts

On each contract, set the ROU asset, accumulated depreciation, interest and depreciation expense accounts plus the payment journal — the Accounting tab guides you.

3
Create your first lease

Click New, enter terms and IBR — initial liability and ROU asset are computed live. Click Activate Lease to generate the schedule.

4
Post entries monthly

Click Post Due Entries: every due period becomes a posted journal entry, marked on the schedule.

FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before installing.

Does this handle IFRS 16 and ASC 842?
Yes. The module applies the IFRS 16 single lessee model and carries an informational ASC 842 finance/operating classification field for US GAAP dual-model disclosure.
Can I change lease terms mid-contract?
Yes. Create a Modification with the new payment, term or IBR: the remaining liability is re-measured, the ROU asset adjusted and the future schedule rebuilt — all documented in the audit trail.
Does it work with Community edition?
Yes. It works on both Odoo Community and Enterprise editions, on Odoo.sh, on-premise and Docker.
How are exempt leases handled?
Short-term (12 months or less) and low-value leases can be flagged as exempt: payments are expensed straight-line with no ROU asset or liability, per IFRS 16 para 5-6.
