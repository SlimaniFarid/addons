Investment Management & Portfolios

Portfolios, investment lines, market-price valuations, dividends and coupons, maturity alerts and PDF performance reports.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Treasury teams track securities and placements in spreadsheets &mdash; no valuation, no dividend/coupon follow-up, no performance view and no maturity alerts.

This module solves it with portfolios, priced lines, computed revenues and daily maturity alerts.

Everything you need

📄

Portfolios & Lines

Stocks, bonds, money market, term deposits and other securities.
📈

Valuations

Market-price history by date with computed values.

💵

Dividends & Coupons

Computed coupon amounts and income tracking.
⚠️

Maturity Alerts

Daily cron alerts for bonds and term deposits.

📄

PDF Reports

Performance report per portfolio and maturity lists.
🛡

Multi-company

User and manager groups with company rules.

How it works

1

Create a portfolio

Company account, bank, currency and responsible.
2

Add investment lines

Quantity, cost and current price; value and latent gain are computed.
3

Record prices & revenues

Valuations by date; dividends and coupons with computed amounts.
4

Monitor & report

Daily maturity alerts and PDF performance reports.
