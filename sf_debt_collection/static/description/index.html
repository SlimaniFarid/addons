Credit & Debt Collection

Aging analysis, collection cases, dunning plans and payment promises &mdash; directly from your Odoo ERP.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Overdue invoices tracked in spreadsheets and emails cost you cash and time, with no aging visibility.

Every open receivable matters, but without structured collection cases, automatic aging and dunning escalation, follow-ups are inconsistent and promises get forgotten. Odoo has no native debt collection &mdash; this module fills that gap.

Everything you need

📈

Aging analysis

Total due and overdue per case, plus days overdue, computed automatically.
📁

Collection cases

One case per customer with collector, priority and workflow.

🔔

Dunning plans

Escalation levels with days-after-due.
📤

Dunning runs

Generate a run per level, draft to sent.

🤝

Payment promises

Record promises, track kept vs broken.
📞

Action log

Calls, emails and letters logged per case.
