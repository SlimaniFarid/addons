Why Credit & Debt Collection?

Recover what you are owed, politely and systematically. This module gives your finance team structured collection cases, automatic aging from real accounting data, dunning escalation and payment promises — all in native Odoo.

✗ The Problem

✕ Overdue invoices tracked in spreadsheets and emails
✕ No aging visibility: who owes what, and for how long
✕ Dunning reminders sent manually, inconsistently
✕ Promises made on the phone and forgotten

✓ The Solution

✓ Collection cases per customer with collector and priority
✓ Automatic aging (total due / overdue) from accounting data
✓ Dunning plans with escalation levels and due dates
✓ Payment promises with follow-up dates and status

Key Features

📊
Aging analysis
Total due and total overdue per case, plus days overdue, computed automatically from your accounting data.
📁
Collection cases
One case per customer, with collector, priority and a clear open → resolved workflow.
🔔
Dunning plans
Escalation levels (reminder, first notice, final notice) each with days-after-due.
📬
Dunning runs
Generate a dunning run per level and track draft → sent → done.
🤝
Payment promises
Record promises with dates and amounts; track kept vs broken.
📞
Action log
Calls, emails, letters and meetings logged on each case with full history.
💳
Credit limits
Partner credit usage and available credit computed live.
🗓️
Next follow-up
Next promise date computed and shown on every case.

Who Is It For?

Finance and credit management teams
SMEs selling on credit who want to reduce late payments
Companies wanting a professional dunning process
Accountants tracking customer risk

How It Works

1
Create a collection case for a customer.
2
Press Refresh Invoices to pull overdue invoices.
3
Log actions and record payment promises.
4
Press Create Dunning to generate the next level.
5
Work the case to resolution and track everything.

Frequently Asked Questions

? Does it read real accounting data?

Yes, freely: levels, order and days-after-due from the UI.
