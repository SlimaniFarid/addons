FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before installing.

How are weights computed?
From product weight and volume fields times delivery quantities.
Can one delivery be on two loads?
No - a picking belongs to one load, keeping manifests clean.
Does it plan pallet patterns?
It counts pallets per delivery; 3D pattern planning is out of scope.
