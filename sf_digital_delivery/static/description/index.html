E-commerce Digital Goods & License Key Delivery

Sell and deliver digital goods (software licenses, activation keys, e-books, downloads) on Odoo.

Features

  • Digital products with license key or download delivery types.
  • Automatic generation of unique license keys.
  • Expirable download links with expiry tracking.
  • Automatic delivery creation on order confirmation.
  • Key lifecycle with activation limits, revocation and expiry.
  • Daily cron and multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Delivery: Draft, Generated, Delivered, Failed, Cancelled.
  • Key: Generated, Delivered, Activated, Revoked, Expired.
