Resource Capacity Planning

Allocate resources to projects, track capacity and avoid overload &mdash; directly from your Odoo ERP.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

No visibility on who is overloaded leads to missed deadlines and burned-out teams.

Workload is balanced in spreadsheets, capacity is unknown until it is too late, and projects slip. Odoo has no native resource capacity planning &mdash; this module fills that gap.

Everything you need

👤

Resources

People, machines and spaces with capacity per day.
📄

Allocations

Link resources to tasks with hours and dates.

📈

Utilization

Capacity vs allocation computed live per resource.
⚠️

Overload alerts

Filter overloaded resources to rebalance quickly.

👑

Teams & types

Group by type and team for clear overviews.
