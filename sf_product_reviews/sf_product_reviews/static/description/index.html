E-commerce Product Reviews & Ratings

Collect, moderate and display product reviews and ratings on Odoo.

Features

  • Customer reviews with rating, title and comment.
  • Moderation workflow: draft, submitted, approved, rejected, archived.
  • Verified purchase detection from confirmed orders.
  • Aggregated average rating and review count per product.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Review: Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Archived.
