Why Marketplace Hub?

Run your own multi-vendor marketplace on top of Odoo. Centralize vendors, product listings, commissions and payouts across all your sales channels — web store, Amazon, eBay or your own B2B platform.

✗ The Problem

✕ Vendors, products and commissions tracked in spreadsheets
✕ No central view of sales across channels
✕ Commissions computed by hand, prone to error
✕ Payouts managed with email and manual transfers

✓ The Solution

✓ One hub for all your marketplaces and channels
✓ Vendors with commission rates and payout accounts
✓ Automatic commission computation on sales
✓ Payout workflow with approval and payment tracking

Frequently Asked Questions

? Can I have several channels?
Yes — one marketplace record per channel, each with its own currency.
? How are commissions computed?
Automatically from the vendor rate and the sold amount of each listing.
? How are vendors paid?
Payouts are created per vendor with an approve → paid workflow.
