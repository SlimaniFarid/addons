HSE Incident & Safety Management

Manage incidents, inspections, risks, permits and PPE for a complete workplace health and safety program.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Workplace incidents and safety issues are still reported on paper &mdash; every delay increases risk and liability.

HSE teams track incidents, inspections and permits in spreadsheets or paper logs. There is no single view of risks, no preventive maintenance of checks and no evidence trail. This module brings a complete health and safety workflow directly into Odoo.

How it works
