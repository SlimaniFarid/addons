Multi-Company Consolidation

Consolidate accounting data across companies and currencies &mdash; a group view your CFO can trust.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Group results are stitched together in Excel from files each entity sends late.

Multi-company groups struggle to see one P&amp;L. Each entity runs its own books and currency; consolidation is a monthly manual ritual. This module brings consolidation periods into Odoo Accounting.
