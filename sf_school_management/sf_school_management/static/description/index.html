School Management & Continuing Education (SIS)

Students, groups/classes, teachers, courses, absences, grades, report cards and tuition fee management.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Students, notes and fees are tracked in spreadsheets &mdash; no global view, billing errors and no automatic report cards.

This module solves it with a dedicated student information system, automatic weighted averages and PDF reports.

📄

🏫

Academic years, classes and teacher supervision.

📜

Grades & Weighted Averages

Coefficients, manager confirmation and averages.
📅

Absences

Reasons and justified/unjustified workflow.

💰

Tuition Fees & Alerts

Payments, amount due and overdue cron alerts.
📈

Dashboard & PDF Reports

Students by group/status, report cards, unpaid fees.

How it works

1

Set up years, groups & teachers

Create the academic year and the classes with their supervisors.
2

Register students & enroll them

Record student identity, tutors and group; activate them.
3

Enter grades & absences

Teachers record grades with coefficients; the manager confirms.
4

Track fees & print reports

Record payments, follow overdue alerts and print report cards.
