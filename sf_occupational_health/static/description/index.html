Occupational Health & Medical Surveillance

Medical files, visits, aptitudes and expiry alerts for every employee.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Medical surveillance dates are tracked manually &mdash; an expired aptitude can block a position.

This module solves it with a dedicated register, automatic checks, alerts and a follow-up dashboard.
