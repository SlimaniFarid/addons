Compliance Register & Document Manager

Track compliance documents, expirations, renewals and audits in a single reliable register.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Compliance documents expire in silence &mdash; one forgotten renewal can cost a licence or a fine.

Certificates, licences, permits and declarations are stored as scattered files. Nobody knows what expires when. This module centralizes the compliance register with expiry alerts, renewal workflow and an audit trail.

Everything you need

📄

Document Register

Certificates, licences, permits with full metadata.
🔔

Expiry Alerts

Automatic warnings before documents expire.

🔄

Renewal Workflow

Renew documents and keep a full history.
📋

Categories & Status

Organize by type, owner and lifecycle status.

📃

Audit History

Complete renewal and change history per document.
⏰

Daily Cron

Automatic expiry alert computation every day.
