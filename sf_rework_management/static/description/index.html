Rework Management

Track rework orders, operations and scrap with cost computation.

Features

  • Rework orders linked to products and lots.
  • Operations with hours and operator assignment.
  • Scrap registration with automatic valuation.
  • Automatic labor, scrap and total cost computation.
  • Workflow: Draft, In Progress, Completed, Closed, Cancelled.
  • Escalation alerts for long-running orders.
  • Rework order PDF.
  • Multi-company support.
