Salon & Beauty Studio Management

Complete management for hairdressing salons and beauty studios.

Features

  • Appointments with staff availability and conflict detection.
  • Per-employee schedules and customer service history.
  • Prepaid packages and memberships.
  • Automatic staff commission calculation.
  • Automatic invoicing of completed services.
  • Daily package expiry handling.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Appointment: Draft, Confirmed, In Progress, Done, Cancelled, No Show.
  • Package: Sold, Partially Used, Exhausted, Expired, Refunded.
  • Commission: Draft, Computed, Paid.
