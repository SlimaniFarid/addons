Retail Store Credit & Customer Wallet

Track reusable store credits, customer balances, usages, adjustments and expirations.

Features

  • Customer credit accounts with automatic balances.
  • Reusable credits with reason, amount and expiration.
  • Partial or full usage on sales.
  • Manager-gated confirmation, adjustment and cancellation.
  • Full movement history per credit.
  • Daily cron for expirations and reminders.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Credit: Draft, Confirmed, Used, Expired, Cancelled, Adjusted.
