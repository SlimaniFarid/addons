HACCP Food Safety

Sanitary compliance for restaurants, food industry and distribution: HACCP plans, monitoring checks, deviations and auditable PDF registers.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Food safety is tracked in paper logs and spreadsheets — auditors cannot verify who checked what, and when.

This module solves it with HACCP plans, automatic deviation detection, corrective actions and an auditable PDF register per site.

Everything you need

📍

HACCP Sites

Sites with prerequisites (cleaning, water, pest, training).
📋

HACCP Plans

Plans per process/zone with steps, hazards and CCP.

🌡

Monitoring Checks

Temperature, cleaning and pH checks with results.
⚠️

Deviation Detection

Automatic flag when results leave the critical range.

🔧

Corrective Actions

Due dates and manager-only closure of nonconformities.
📄

HACCP Register PDF

Auditable PDF register generated per site.

How it works

1

Configure sites & prerequisites

Define sites and their prerequisites (cleaning, water, pest, training, waste, storage).
2

Define HACCP plans

Add steps, hazards, CCP and critical limits per process or zone.
3

Record monitoring checks

Log temperature, cleaning and pH results; deviations are detected automatically.
4

Resolve & audit

Fill corrective actions, close nonconformities and generate the PDF register.
