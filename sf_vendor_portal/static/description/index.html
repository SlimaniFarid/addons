Vendor Portal & e-Procurement

Self-service vendor portal: RFQs, quotations, orders and invoices online &mdash; directly from your Odoo ERP.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

RFQs exchanged back and forth by email, with no visibility on vendor responses and no self-service for suppliers.

Procurement teams chase vendors by phone, responses live in inboxes, and suppliers have no self-service view of their documents. Odoo has no native vendor portal &mdash; this module fills that gap.

Everything you need

🔒

Secure vendor access

Each vendor gets a portal login for their own documents only.
📄

RFQ & quotations

Vendors view their requests for quotation with lines and totals.

✔

Accept / decline

One click to accept, decline or counter-offer.
📦

Confirmed orders

Purchase orders visible with full line details.

💳

Invoices & payments

Vendor sees invoices and payment status online.
👋

Automatic welcome

Welcome message with a secure sign-in link.

How it works

1

Enable the portal

Turn on portal access for a supplier.
2

The vendor receives a sign-in link.
3

Vendor responds

Accept, decline or counter-offer online.
4

Track responses

See every response in Odoo.
