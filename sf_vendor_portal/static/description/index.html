Vendor Portal & e-Procurement

Self-service vendor portal: RFQs, quotations, orders and invoices online.

Odoo 18 Odoo 19 99.75 € OPL-1

Let your suppliers collaborate online instead of exchanging emails. This module gives every vendor a secure self-service portal to see their quotations, accept or decline RFQs, propose counter-offers and track orders and invoices.

Why you need it

The problem

  • RFQs exchanged back and forth by email
  • No visibility on vendor responses
  • Vendors chase status by phone
  • No self-service for suppliers

The solution

  • Secure self-service portal for each vendor
  • Vendors see quotations, orders and invoices
  • Accept, decline or counter-offer from the portal
  • Automatic welcome and notifications

Key features

🔐

Secure vendor access

Each vendor gets a portal login with access to their own documents only.

📄

RFQ & quotations

Vendors view their requests for quotation with lines, prices and totals.

✅

Accept / decline

One click to accept, decline or propose a counter-offer on a quotation.

📦

Confirmed orders

Purchases orders visible to the vendor with full line details.

💳

Invoices & payments

Vendor sees their invoices and payment status online.

👋

Automatic welcome

Welcome message with a secure sign-in link when the portal is enabled.

⚙️

Portal settings

Auto-create portal users and notify vendors on new RFQs.

📊

Vendor dashboard

Summary of quotations, orders and responses on the portal home.

Who it's for

  • Purchasing departments wanting to streamline RFQs
  • Companies moving away from email-based procurement
  • Vendors wanting self-service access to their documents
  • Businesses with many suppliers to manage

How it works

  1. Install the module and open Vendor Portal settings.
  2. Enable the vendor portal on a supplier.
  3. The vendor receives a welcome message with sign-in link.
  4. The vendor accepts, declines or counters your RFQs online.
  5. You track every response directly in Odoo.
