Training & Certification Tracking

Centralize trainings, sessions, registrations and certifications — never miss an expiry again.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0 / 19.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Expired certificates and untracked mandatory trainings are a compliance accident waiting to happen.

Safety, hygiene and competence certificates expire silently. Mandatory trainings are not followed, and ISO / regulatory audits demand centralized proof. This module brings structured certification tracking and a compliance matrix directly into Odoo.
