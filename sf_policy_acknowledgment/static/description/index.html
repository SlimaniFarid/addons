Company Policy & Employee Acknowledgment Register

Versioned internal policies, employee assignment and acknowledgment sign-off.

Features

  • Versioned policies with category, dates and owner.
  • Publication workflow: Draft, Published, Retired, Archived.
  • Automatic acknowledgment register at publication.
  • Employee sign-off with date and user tracking.
  • Acknowledgment coverage rate.
  • Automatic reminders for pending and expiring policies.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Policy: Draft, Published, Retired, Archived.
  • Acknowledgment: Pending, Acknowledged.
  • Daily crons raise acknowledgment and expiry reminders.
