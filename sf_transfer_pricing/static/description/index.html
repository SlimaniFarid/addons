FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before installing.

Does it compute prices automatically on invoices?
It analyses posted intercompany invoices against the applicable policy and flags variances - pricing decisions stay under your control, with evidence.
Which OECD methods are covered?
CUP, Cost-Plus, Resale-Minus and TNMM, with markup/margin parameters and benchmarking notes per policy.
Does it produce Master File and Local File?
It maintains the documentation register (sections, owners, review dates, status) and stores supporting files - the structure auditors expect.
Does it work with Community edition?
Yes, Community and Enterprise on Odoo.sh, on-premise and Docker.
