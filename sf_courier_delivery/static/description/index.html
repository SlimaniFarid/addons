Courier & Parcel Delivery Management

Courier operations for courier and express delivery companies.

Features

  • Pickup/delivery requests with addresses and time windows.
  • Courier assignment and daily route planning.
  • Delivery tracking: assigned, in transit, delivered, failed, returned.
  • Proof of delivery (signature or photo) with timestamp.
  • Failure handling with retry and return flow.
  • Daily alerts for overdue and unresolved deliveries.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Request: Draft, Confirmed, Done, Cancelled.
  • Delivery: Draft, Assigned, In Transit, Delivered, Failed, Returned, Cancelled.
  • Route: Draft, Planned, In Progress, Done, Cancelled.
