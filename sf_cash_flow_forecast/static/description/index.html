A liquidity gap can undo months of good sales. This module turns your real Odoo accounting data into a rolling cash forecast — receivables, payables and bank position — with low-balance alerts before the gap becomes critical.

Why you need it

The problem

  • You invoice well but cash arrives late — you don’t know when
  • Supplier bills pile up with no payment plan
  • Spreadsheet forecasts are outdated the moment they are shared
  • Overdrafts and emergency loans eat your margin

The solution

  • Automatic forecast from open invoices, by due date
  • Outflows from vendor bills and confirmed purchase orders
  • Manual lines for planned payments, loans and transfers
  • Low-balance alerts before the gap becomes critical

Key features

💼

Rolling cash position

Opening balance plus expected inflows and outflows, across your bank and cash journals.

📥

Receivables forecast

Open customer invoices pulled automatically with their maturity date.

📤

Payables forecast

Vendor bills and confirmed purchase orders turned into dated outflows.

✍️

Manual entries

Planned payments, loan drawdowns, transfers and capex in one click.

🚨

Low-balance alerts

A threshold flags every day your projected balance falls below it.

📅

Configurable horizon

Project 7 to 180 days ahead; see the minimum balance and its date.

Who it's for

  • CFOs and financial controllers
  • Small and medium businesses avoiding overdrafts
  • Growing companies planning investment and hiring
  • Accounting firms delivering forecasts to clients

How it works

  1. Install and open Accounting > Cash Flow.
  2. Create a forecast: start date, horizon, bank/cash journals.
  3. Generate Lines — receivables, payables and POs are pulled automatically.
  4. Add manual lines for one-off flows.
  5. Set an alert threshold and confirm; alerts flag risky days.
  6. Review daily and regenerate whenever data changes.
