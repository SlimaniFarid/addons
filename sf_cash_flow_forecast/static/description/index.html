Why Cash Flow & Treasury Manager?

Know your cash position weeks ahead. This module builds automatic cash flow forecasts from your receivables, payables and purchase orders, and alerts you to shortages.

✗ The Problem

✕ Cash position unknown until the bank statement arrives
✕ Shortages discovered too late to act
✕ Forecasts built manually in spreadsheets
✕ No single view of inflows and outflows

✓ The Solution

✓ Automatic forecast from real accounting data
✓ Opening, closing and minimum balance computed live
✓ Shortage alerts with configurable thresholds
✓ Manual lines for planned receipts and payments
