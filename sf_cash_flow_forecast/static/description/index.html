Cash Flow & Treasury Manager

Forecast, monitor and secure your company cash flow weeks ahead &mdash; directly from your Odoo ERP.

✓ Plug & PlayOdoo 18.0Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Not knowing your cash position weeks ahead leaves you reacting to shortages instead of preventing them.

Cash crunches arrive when nobody saw them coming. Forecasts are rebuilt in spreadsheets every week, receivables and payables are spread across modules, and the minimum balance is a mystery until the bank statement lands. Odoo has no native cash flow forecast &mdash; this module fills that gap.

Everything you need

💰

Cash Forecast

Automatic inflows and outflows from your accounting data.
📅

Horizon Planning

Forecast over a configurable number of days ahead.

⚠️

Shortage Alerts

Automatic alerts when the balance goes below threshold.
📥

Auto Lines

Receivables, payables and purchase orders included automatically.

✎

Manual Lines

Add planned receipts and payments by hand.
💵

Bank View

Consolidated forecast across your bank journals.
