Cash Flow & Treasury Manager

Forecast your cash position, track receivables and payables, and avoid liquidity gaps.

Key Features

  • Rolling cash position with opening balance
  • Automatic inflow forecast from customer invoices by due date
  • Automatic outflow forecast from vendor bills and purchase orders
  • Manual cash flow lines for planned payments, loans, transfers
  • Configurable horizon (7 to 180 days)
  • Low-balance alerts and projected minimum balance

Works with native Odoo accounting data. Install and open the Cash Flow dashboard.
