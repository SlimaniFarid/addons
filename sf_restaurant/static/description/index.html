Restaurant, Cafe & In-Room Dining

Restaurant and cafe dining room management for restaurant managers and head waiters.

Features

  • Tables and zones with status tracking (free, reserved, occupied, cleaning).
  • Reservations with capacity control without overbooking.
  • Menu categories and items with per-service availability.
  • Table orders with kitchen ticket transmission and preparation validation.
  • Daily revenue tracking by service and by table.
  • Daily reminder cron activities for reservations and occupied tables.
  • Multi-company support with manager-only reserved actions.
  • PDF reports: kitchen ticket, table bill and daily revenue report.

Workflows

  • Table: Free, Reserved, Occupied, Cleaning.
  • Reservation: Draft, Confirmed, Seated, Done, Cancelled.
  • Order: Draft, Transmitted, Prepared, Served, Closed, Cancelled.
