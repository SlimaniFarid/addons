Freight Audit

Audit carrier invoices against your negotiated contracts and real shipments. Detect overcharges, manage disputes, and recover the 3-10% of freight spend that leaks away every year.

How it works

  1. Import carrier invoice CSV files with dry-run validation.
  2. The engine matches each line to real shipments and applies your contract rules.
  3. Typed findings (rate variance, unauthorized surcharge, duplicate billing, phantom shipment...) are flagged with severity levels.
  4. Create a dispute, submit it, track the carrier response, and resolve by generating a reconciled vendor credit note.
  5. Payment is blocked while findings or disputes remain open.
