Utility & Sub-Meter Billing

Billing for water, energy and sub-metering companies.

Features

  • Delivery points and meters registry.
  • Reading campaigns with manual entry and CSV import.
  • Automatic consumption calculation.
  • Tiered tariff grids.
  • Consumption invoices with accounting posting.
  • Abnormal consumption and overdue detection.
  • Multi-company support.

Workflows

  • Reading: Draft, Done, Validated, Rejected.
  • Campaign: Draft, Open, Closed, Cancelled.
  • Invoice: Draft, Posted, Paid, Overdue, Cancelled.
