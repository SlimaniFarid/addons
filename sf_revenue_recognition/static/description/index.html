Revenue Recognition & Subscription Billing

ASC 606 / IFRS 15 compliant revenue recognition

Key Features

  • Performance Obligations — Identify & separate obligations in contracts
  • Standalone Selling Price (SSP) — Observable inputs, residual allocation
  • Revenue Allocation — Proportional allocation to obligations
  • Recognition Methods — Over time (POC) or point in time
  • Deferral Schedules — Automated with percentage-of-completion
  • Contract Modifications — Variable consideration, renewals, upsells
  • Disclosure Reports — Contract assets/liabilities, remaining obligations
  • Audit Trail — Full journal entry linkage

Compatibility

Odoo 18.0 and 19.0. Check module dependencies for required apps.
